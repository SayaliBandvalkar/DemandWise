# DemandWise — Model Card

## 1. Model Details
- **Model Name:** DemandWise Hierarchical Forecasting & Reconciliation Engine
- **Version:** 1.0.0
- **Model Type:** Hybrid Hierarchical Forecaster (Statistical Holt-Winters + Croston SBA + LightGBM Autoregressive Trees + Linear MinT Reconciliation + Split Conformal Intervals)
- **License:** MIT License (Academic Capstone MDS-07)
- **Authors:** M.Sc. Data Science Candidate

---

## 2. Intended Use
- **Primary Use Case:** Enterprise multi-echelon retail demand planning, supply chain replenishment, and safety stock sizing across nested product and store hierarchies.
- **Out-of-Scope Uses:** High-frequency intraday financial trading; real-time critical medical equipment allocation without human review; demand series with less than 14 days of historical observations.

---

## 3. Training & Validation Data
- **Dataset:** 365 days of deterministic retail hierarchy transactions across 48 bottom-level SKU-store combinations, 4 categories, 4 stores, and 2 geographical operating regions.
- **Data Preprocessing:** Missing-date forward padding, zero-demand retention (for Croston intermittent handling), calendar extraction (day-of-week, month, weekend flags), and shifted autoregressive lag generation.
- **Data Splits:** Purely chronological forward-chaining splits without lookahead leakage.

---

## 4. Evaluation Factors & Metrics
- **Point Accuracy:** MAE, RMSE, MASE, RMSSE, Bias, WAPE.
- **Hierarchical Coherence:** Maximum and Mean Absolute Coherence Discrepancy: $|y_t - S b_t|$.
- **Interval Calibration:** Empirical Coverage Rate (ECR) at 80% and 95% nominal levels, Mean Interval Width (MIW), Winkler Interval Score.
- **Operational Performance:** Total relevant inventory cost, Stockout days, and Customer service fill rate.

---

## 5. Quantitative Performance Summary
- **Top-Level MASE:** 0.729 (Exponential Smoothing) / 0.985 (LightGBM)
- **Coherence Error:** Reduced from 112.94 units (Unreconciled) to 0.0000 units (Bottom-Up & Top-Down)
- **Prediction Interval Coverage:** 95.5% empirical coverage achieved via Split Conformal Prediction against a 95.0% nominal target.

---

## 6. Limitations & Ethical Considerations
- **Non-Stationarity:** Models assume bounded distributional shifts. Rapid structural regime shifts (e.g., pandemic supply chain lockdowns) require immediate retraining or manual intervention.
- **Zero-Demand Intermittency:** While Croston SBA handles lumpiness effectively, SKU lines with 100% zero demand over long periods may cause degraded parameter estimates.
- **Human Oversight:** Inventory order quantities generated by DemandWise should serve as decision-support suggestions for category planners rather than fully automated unmonitored purchasing.
